# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release. Six roles (Werewolf, Villager, Seer, Doctor, Witch, Hunter) for 4 to 16 players.
- Secret roles and night actions are sealed per player on the board; town chat is signed, and the dead cannot speak.
- Shared table on the board, and a panel for phones and desktops.
- English, Thai, and Simplified Chinese, chosen per player. Role cards carry localized banners.
